Fetched 2026-08-16. General terms governing use of Google APIs by client
applications ("API Clients").

## Attested details

1. Section 2(c) "Permitted Access": "You will only access (or attempt to access) an API by the means described in the documentation of that API... You will not misrepresent or mask either your identity or your API Client's identity when using the APIs or developer accounts."
2. Section 3(a): "YOU AGREE THAT GOOGLE MAY MONITOR USE OF THE APIS TO ENSURE QUALITY, IMPROVE GOOGLE PRODUCTS AND SERVICES, AND VERIFY YOUR COMPLIANCE WITH THE TERMS... Google may suspend access to the APIs by you or your API Client without notice if we reasonably believe that you are in violation of the Terms."
3. Section 4(b) "Confidential Matters": "Developer credentials (such as passwords, keys, and client IDs) are intended to be used by you and identify your API Client. You will keep your credentials confidential and make reasonable efforts to prevent and discourage other API Clients from using your credentials. Developer credentials may not be embedded in open source projects."
4. Section 4(a) "API Prohibitions" includes not interfering with or disrupting the APIs or the servers or networks providing them, and not reverse engineering or attempting to extract source code from any API or related software except where such restriction is prohibited by applicable law.
