## Attested details

1. The February 2026 account disruptions were "the result of a series of Antigravity bans rolled out to address violations of the Antigravity Terms of Service (ToS), specifically the use of 3rd party tools or proxies to access Antigravity resources and quotas."
2. Because abuse prevention happens at a shared backend layer, Antigravity bans also blocked access to Gemini CLI and Gemini Code Assist; Google conducted a "system-wide automated unban" for recently flagged accounts, restoring access within a day or two.
3. Going forward, a flagged account receives an email and a specific in-product error pointing to a Google Form; submitting a recertification acknowledging that "bypassing system measures or circumventing usage limits is prohibited" triggers automatic reinstatement, typically within a day or two.
4. "If an account is flagged for a second violation of ToS, it will be permanently banned."
5. "Using third-party software, tools, or services to harvest or piggyback on Gemini CLI's OAuth authentication to access our backend services is a direct violation of Gemini CLI's applicable terms and policies."
