# Research conventions

Research in this repository follows the Workbench research discipline.

- Attest fetched sources under `.research/attestations/` before citing them.
- Cite attested details as `[source-handle]{N}`.
- Keep source-faithful observations in attestations and project decisions in briefs or Workbench items.
- Prefer stable primary-source URLs and current immutable revisions.
- Do not record credentials, personal data, health data, or session material.
- Validate research and rebuild `.knowledge/index.json` with the Workbench research scripts after changes.
